Who these terms are between

These terms apply between you ("the client") and AntherLink, currently operated by Muhammad Abdullah and Sher-Ali, based in Lahore, Pakistan, when you engage us for a website, software, automation, design, video, or advertising project.

How a project starts

A project begins once we've agreed on scope and either side has confirmed it in writing, typically over email. Specific pricing, deliverables, and timelines are set per project and are not published on this site because they vary by scope.

Payment

Payment schedule and accepted methods are agreed per project until a standard policy is published here. Any deposit or milestone terms will be stated in writing before work begins.

Revisions and scope changes

Work is scoped up front. Requests outside that scope are treated as additional work and quoted separately before we proceed, so costs don't appear unexpectedly.

Ownership

Once a project is paid in full, ownership of the final deliverables (site, code, designs, or assets created specifically for you) transfers to you, unless we've agreed otherwise in writing. AntherLink may keep and reuse general techniques, non-client-specific code, and know-how developed during the project.

What we don't guarantee

We build things to work correctly and to reasonable performance and accessibility standards, but we can't guarantee specific business outcomes such as sales, rankings, or traffic, since those depend on many factors outside our control.

Cancellation

Either side can end a project in progress. You're responsible for paying for work completed up to that point; we'll hand over what's been built so far.

Limitation of liability

AntherLink's liability for any claim related to a project is limited to the amount paid for that project. This is a standard limitation and will be reviewed with a lawyer as the business formalizes.

Governing law

Governing jurisdiction will be confirmed once AntherLink completes SECP registration. Until then, disputes are handled directly and in good faith.
