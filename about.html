About AntherLink

We started AntherLink because most small business sites deserve better than a template.

Founded in Lahore, working with businesses across Pakistan, the Gulf, North America, Europe, and beyond.

How we started

AntherLink was founded by Muhammad Abdullah and Sher-Ali, officially starting January 1, 2026, out of Lahore, Pakistan. The team works remotely and takes on projects for businesses starting out online as well as ones already running a site that no longer fits what they've grown into.

What we work on

Most projects fall into one or more of six areas: custom websites and software, AI automation, UI/UX design, 3D and product visuals, video editing, and lead generation with ads. We'd rather do fewer things well for a client than hand off a generic package.

Where we work

We're starting with clients in the USA, UAE, UK, Canada, Europe, Oman, China, Japan, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Indonesia, Australia, and Pakistan, and plan to take on more countries as the studio grows.

Where we're headed

AntherLink is a young studio. We're currently working toward formal registration as a private limited company with Pakistan's Securities and Exchange Commission (SECP). Until that's complete, AntherLink operates as a founder-led team rather than a registered company. See our terms for what that means for client agreements right now.

Want to talk through a project?

Tell us what's not working with your current site or software, and we'll tell you honestly whether we're a fit.
