Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Draft notice: AntherLink is not yet a formally registered company (see Terms of Service). This policy describes our current data practices in good faith and will be revised once the business is SECP-registered and once any additional tools (analytics, forms, hosting) are finalized. Review with a lawyer before relying on this as a binding legal document.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to visitors of antherlink.vercel.app and to people who contact AntherLink about a project. AntherLink is currently operated by Muhammad Abdullah and Sher-Ali, based in Lahore, Pakistan.

Information we collect

We collect information in two ways:

  • Information you give us directly: your name, email address, business name, and project details, when you submit the contact form or email us.
  • Information collected automatically: basic, aggregated visit data such as page views and referring pages, collected through Vercel Web Analytics. This analytics tool does not use cookies and does not track individuals across sites.

How we use it

We use the information you submit to respond to your inquiry, prepare a quote, and deliver a project if we're engaged. We use aggregated analytics data to understand which pages are useful and to fix broken parts of the site. We do not sell your information to third parties.

Cookies

This site does not currently set marketing or tracking cookies. If that changes, for example if we add a tool that requires cookies, we will update this policy and add a consent banner before that tool is enabled.

Third-party tools

We currently use Vercel for hosting and analytics. As the studio adds tools (for example, a form backend or a CRM), this section will be updated to list them and how they handle data.

How long we keep information

We keep contact form submissions and project correspondence for as long as needed to respond to you or deliver a project, and delete or archive them afterward. (Exact retention periods are still being defined as we formalize internal processes.)

Your choices

You can ask us to delete information you've sent us, or ask what we hold about you, by emailing abdullahcms.dev@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

AntherLink's services are intended for business owners and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We'll update the date at the top of this page when this policy changes, and post material changes here before they take effect.
